# Daily Global Finance Report Style Guide

## Sunday and Monday Morning Issue-Focused Reports

Use this rule when the report is written on Sunday morning or Monday morning KST, because there is usually no new U.S. regular-session close after the prior Friday close. These reports should not simply repeat Friday's market recap. Their main job is to analyze major non-market-session news that could affect stocks.

The title must still start with `데일리 리포트`.

### Sunday Morning KST

Use an issue-focused weekend review format.

Recommended title:

`# 데일리 리포트 - 주말 이슈 점검 (YYYY년 M월 D일)`

Main purpose:

- Make it clear that the market-price basis is the previous Friday U.S. close, but the report focus is weekend issue analysis.
- Focus on major political, geopolitical, policy, regulatory, macro, corporate, AI/technology, energy, credit, and social events that did not fit into the normal market recap.
- For each issue, explain why it matters to equities, which sectors/assets are most exposed, and what investors should check when markets reopen.
- Keep U.S. major index and global index tables short, labeled as `전거래일 마감 기준`, and do not make them the center of the report.
- Replace intraday-style sections with:
  1. 한 줄 결론
  2. 주말 핵심 이슈 요약
  3. 전거래일 미국 주요 지수
  4. 글로벌 참고 지수
  5. 정치·정책 이슈와 증시 영향
  6. 지정학·에너지 이슈와 증시 영향
  7. 기업·산업 뉴스와 주가 영향
  8. 매크로·금리·환율 점검
  9. 월요일 개장 전 관찰 포인트
  10. 한국시장 체크포인트
  11. 결론
- The additional report for U.S. interesting/volatile top 10 stocks remains required, but explain that the list is based on Friday close plus weekend news flow and issue sensitivity.

### Monday Morning KST

Use a weekend-news impact and week-ahead preview format.

Recommended title:

`# 데일리 리포트 - 월요일 이슈 프리뷰 (YYYY년 M월 D일)`

Main purpose:

- Make it clear that the market-price basis is the previous Friday U.S. close unless fresh futures/pre-market data is explicitly available and timestamped.
- Focus on what happened over the weekend and how it could affect Monday/weekly equity trading.
- Prioritize news that was not fully reflected in Friday's close: politics, elections, tariffs, sanctions, wars, central bank comments, fiscal policy, regulation, major corporate announcements, lawsuits, M&A, cybersecurity, AI policy, energy disruption, and credit/financial stability.
- For each issue, include:
  - what happened
  - why it matters to stocks
  - likely winners/losers by sector or asset
  - what price action or data to check first
- Keep index tables short and avoid repeating the same Friday close interpretation from Sunday unless new news changed the setup.
- Recommended sections:
  1. 한 줄 결론
  2. 주말 동안 발생한 핵심 이슈
  3. 전거래일 미국 주요 지수
  4. 글로벌 참고 지수
  5. 정치·정책 뉴스의 증시 영향
  6. 지정학·원자재 뉴스의 증시 영향
  7. 기업·산업 뉴스의 증시 영향
  8. 이번 주 주요 이벤트 캘린더
  9. 월요일 개장 체크: 선물, 금리, 달러, 유가, 시장 폭
  10. 한국시장 개장 체크
  11. 리스크 레이더
  12. 결론
- The additional report for U.S. interesting/volatile top 10 stocks remains required, but emphasize "주말 이슈 반영 시 이번 주 관찰 우선순위" instead of "당일 변동성".

### General Weekend Wording

Prefer:

- `전거래일 기준`
- `금요일 미국 정규장 마감 기준`
- `주말 동안 발생한 이슈`
- `주가에 미칠 영향`
- `이번 주 확인할 포인트`
- `월요일 개장 전 체크`
- `정치·정책 리스크`
- `지정학·원자재 리스크`

Avoid:

- `오늘 급등락`
- `장중`
- `오늘 주식시장에 영향을 준`
- any wording that implies a fresh U.S. regular-session close occurred when it did not.

### Issue Selection Priority

Prioritize issues by expected market impact, not by general news popularity.

High priority:

- U.S. politics, fiscal policy, tariffs, sanctions, antitrust, technology regulation, healthcare policy, bank/credit regulation.
- Central bank speeches, inflation/labor surprises released during non-U.S.-session hours, major bond/FX moves.
- Wars, ceasefires, attacks on energy/logistics routes, oil and gas supply disruption, strategic commodity restrictions.
- Major company-specific events: earnings preannouncements, guidance changes, M&A, lawsuits, investigations, cyber incidents, product delays, export restrictions.
- AI/semiconductor policy, cloud/data center capex, power/grid constraints, export controls.
- Korea-relevant issues: semiconductors, batteries, defense, shipbuilding, FX, China/U.S. policy, major Korean ADR or supply-chain news.

Low priority unless market-moving:

- General political commentary without policy consequences.
- Human-interest or social stories with no clear sector/asset channel.
- Meme-stock chatter without broad market relevance.
